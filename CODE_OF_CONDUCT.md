# Code of Conduct  
**SERA – Scientific Extraction & Research Agent**

This project follows a strict, respectful, and inclusive Code of Conduct based on the Contributor Covenant 2.1.  
Our mission is to maintain a collaborative, research-oriented environment that welcomes contributors of all backgrounds while upholding high technical and ethical standards.

---

## 1. Our Pledge

We as contributors, maintainers, and users pledge to create a harassment-free, respectful, and supportive environment for everyone.  
This includes all identities and backgrounds—regardless of:

- Age  
- Disability  
- Gender identity or expression  
- Level of experience  
- Nationality or residence  
- Profession or academic background  
- Socio-economic status  
- Technology choices  

We commit to an open, welcoming space where cutting-edge research, engineering excellence, and professional conduct thrive.

---

## 2. Our Standards

### **Positive Examples of Behavior**
- Demonstrating professionalism, empathy, patience, and kindness.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Acknowledging the work and contributions of others.
- Respecting diverse technical opinions and research directions.
- Focusing conversations on the engineering or scientific problem at hand.
- Actively helping newcomers learn SERA’s architecture and development workflow.

### **Unacceptable Behavior**
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments of any kind.
- Personal attacks or political/religious arguments unrelated to the project.
- Sexualized or violent language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Knowingly spreading misinformation about research or system design.
- Dismissing contributions without justification, or sabotage of project quality.
- Any behavior that reduces the quality, safety, or ethical integrity of SERA.

---

## 3. Responsibilities of Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing contributions fairly and respectfully.
- Taking corrective action when inappropriate behavior occurs.
- Moderating discussions and collaboration spaces (GitHub, issues, PRs).
- Protecting contributors from abuse, harassment, and unethical conduct.
- Ensuring that code, tests, models, and scientific content meet project quality standards.

Maintainers have the right to remove or edit any content violating this Code.

---

## 4. Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by:

- Opening an issue (if appropriate)  
- Contacting a maintainer privately  
- Submitting anonymous feedback (if available)

All complaints will be reviewed fairly, promptly, and confidentially.

Maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality regarding the reporter of an incident.

---

## 5. Enforcement Guidelines

### **Correction**
Minor violations → reminder of expected behavior.

### **Warning**
Significant or repeated violations → formal warning with consequences.

### **Temporary Ban**
Behavior that negatively impacts the project → temporary removal from community interactions.

### **Permanent Ban**
Severe or repeated abusive behavior → permanent exclusion.

---

## 6. Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces:

- GitHub issues, PRs, and discussions  
- Documentation, comments, commit messages  
- Project-related events or calls  
- Interactions representing the project, even offline
